HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1
    Pick your side

    Call Heads (gold coin, marked H) or Tails (silver coin, marked T) before the toss.

  2. 2
    Throw the coin

    Three ways, all the same toss.

  3. 3
    Build a streak

    Every correct call adds one to your streak. A single miss knocks it back to zero.

  4. 4
    Hit the jackpot

    Every 5 correct calls in a row pays out: coins rain down and the game holds still for the fanfare. Keep the streak alive and it pays again at 10, 15, 20…

  5. 5
    Beat your record

    Your longest streak is kept as BEST, saved under your name on this device, so it survives a refresh.
